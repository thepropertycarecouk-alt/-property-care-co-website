1. Our relationship

PCCO Stays, a service of The Property Care Co. (PCCO), acts as an accommodation sourcing and booking intermediary connecting accommodation providers (Property Partners) with prospective guests, contractors, businesses and relocation customers. This agreement covers the PCCO Stays property network only. It is separate from any PCCO Host Solutions website design, hosting or other service agreement.

The Property Partner for this bespoke agreement is Dimi Boutique Stays Ltd, acting in respect of The Windsor Nook and any further property expressly accepted in writing. This is a non-exclusive commercial arrangement; no employment, tenancy, joint venture or property management contract is created. The Property Partner controls its property, prices and booking acceptance.

2. Non-exclusive partnership and independent bookings

Dimi Boutique Stays Ltd may continue advertising and taking bookings via its website, Airbnb, Booking.com, Uplisting and other channels. PCCO has no commission entitlement to bookings generated independently from customers PCCO has not introduced or assisted. A listing on PCCO Stays does not guarantee any booking, occupancy or income.

3. Commission and founding rate

The PCCO commission rate for this Property Partner is 7.85% plus VAT on the commission (not 7.85% plus VAT on the total booking value) for qualifying completed bookings introduced or arranged by PCCO. This rate is fixed for the first 12 months from acceptance of these Terms. The Property Partner provides a total quoted accommodation price inclusive of PCCO's commission, and PCCO deducts its agreed commission from that price before remitting the balance; PCCO will not add its commission to the agreed guest quote without agreement.

Commission is calculated on the final payable accommodation amount for a qualifying booking, excluding refundable security deposits, refunded amounts and any separately identified cleaning fee that PCCO and the Property Partner agreed in writing to exclude before booking confirmation. No commission is charged on a fully refunded cancelled booking. There are no joining, listing, monthly subscription or administration fees under this PCCO Stays agreement.

4. Commission review after twelve months

After the initial 12-month fixed-rate period, PCCO may propose a different rate in response to market conditions, operating costs or industry changes. PCCO will give at least 30 days' written notice before any change takes effect. Any change applies to new bookings confirmed after the stated effective date and does not retrospectively change the rate for bookings already confirmed.

If the Property Partner does not wish to accept the new rate, it may end the partnership by written notice without a termination penalty, subject to confirmed bookings and outstanding obligations. The original 7.85% plus VAT rate remains applicable to bookings confirmed before the revised rate comes into force.

5. Guest payments and payouts

Where PCCO collects guest funds for a confirmed booking, PCCO will initiate payment of the Property Partner's amount by bank transfer within 24 hours of PCCO receiving cleared funds from the guest, after deduction of agreed PCCO commission, VAT and any applicable processing fee. The obligation applies to each cleared instalment received, not to instalments the guest has not yet paid.

PCCO will provide an itemised statement of the booking and deductions. Bank-to-bank processing or circumstances outside PCCO's control may affect when funds appear in the Property Partner's account; PCCO will promptly notify the Property Partner of any known problem. Where the Property Partner collects funds directly, the parties will agree how PCCO's commission is invoiced and paid before the booking is confirmed.

6. Payment processing and other charges

The separate 1.35% processing charge applies only where a guest chooses to pay PCCO by credit card. It is calculated on the amount actually paid by credit card and deducted from the host payout as a business-to-business processing cost, not charged as a guest card surcharge. It does not apply to guest bank transfers, debit-card payments or other payment methods under this bespoke agreement. Most PCCO bookings are ordinarily paid by bank transfer.

Refundable security deposits and refunded accommodation amounts are excluded from the calculation of this separate 1.35% charge, except to the extent of an actual non-refundable external processing cost that has been disclosed and agreed before the relevant transaction. Apart from PCCO's agreed commission, VAT applicable to commission, and the conditional credit-card processing charge described here, PCCO charges no other fees for this property network arrangement.

7. Customer introductions: agreed 90-day exception

PCCO ordinarily seeks a longer protection period for customers introduced through its sourcing activities. As a specific exception for Dimi Boutique Stays Ltd, the parties agree a 90-day introduction-protection period beginning on the date PCCO first introduces the identifiable guest, company or customer to the Property Partner.

The original booking that results from a PCCO introduction remains commissionable even if the parties subsequently complete it directly to avoid PCCO. A substitute booking, extension or repeat booking with that introduced customer that is confirmed during the 90-day protection period also remains commissionable, including where the stay itself takes place after day 90. PCCO may claim only the agreed commission, not an additional penalty.

After the 90-day period, a new repeat booking arranged directly between that customer and Dimi Boutique Stays Ltd without PCCO's involvement does not incur PCCO commission. If PCCO separately sources, negotiates or facilitates a new booking after that date, that PCCO-arranged booking remains commissionable on its own merits. PCCO has no commission entitlement to unrelated independently sourced customers.

8. Enquiries, prices and booking confirmations

PCCO may offer booking opportunities to the Property Partner, which remains free to accept or decline each enquiry. An enquiry, proposed date or website listing does not constitute confirmed availability or a confirmed booking. Before any booking is confirmed, the parties will agree the stay dates, guest numbers, accommodation price, cleaning and other included services, deposit (if any), cancellation and refund rules and payment arrangements.

Property-specific terms communicated and agreed before confirmation form part of that booking. Neither party may unilaterally amend a confirmed booking's material financial terms.

9. Cancellations, cleaning fees and refunds

The agreed cancellation policy for each booking must be given to the guest before confirmation. If a cancelled booking is refunded in full, PCCO charges no commission on it. Where a booking is partly refunded, the qualifying commissionable accommodation amount is reduced by that refund. Any separately itemised cleaning fee may be excluded from commission if agreed in writing with PCCO before the booking is confirmed.

The parties will coordinate promptly on payment reversals, disputed payments or changes to bookings and will provide relevant records to resolve the amount properly due. Neither party can promise a refund on behalf of the other outside agreed booking terms without approval.

10. Refundable security deposits

The Property Partner may request a modest, reasonable refundable security deposit from guests if it wishes. The amount, permitted deductions, evidence process, who holds it and refund timing must be communicated and agreed with the guest before the booking is confirmed. The deposit is not accommodation revenue and is excluded from PCCO commission while refundable.

Unless specifically agreed otherwise in writing, collection, safeguarding, reconciliation and release of the security deposit remain the Property Partner's responsibility. Deductions for alleged damage must be supported by appropriate evidence and follow applicable law and the guest's agreed terms.

11. Guest damage, disputes, unpaid bookings and complaints

PCCO will provide reasonable assistance with booking communications, guest complaints, disputed booking details, payment follow-up and coordination of complaints involving customers introduced by PCCO. PCCO will share relevant information available to it and assist with contacting the guest where appropriate, but it does not act as the property insurer or a guarantor of guest payment.

The Property Partner remains responsible for property upkeep, guest conduct on site, cleaning, maintenance, safety, insurance, damage claims and the operation of the accommodation. PCCO is responsible for losses caused by its own acts, omissions or breaches to the extent required by applicable law. No clause excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

12. Property standards and partner responsibilities

The Property Partner confirms that it has authority to offer the property for guest stays and will maintain all relevant licences, consents, appropriate insurance, gas and electrical safety arrangements, fire precautions, alarms and other applicable legal requirements. It will supply the amenities, bed arrangements, parking, cleaning, utilities and facilities expressly agreed for each confirmed booking.

The Property Partner must notify PCCO promptly of material availability changes, safety issues or other circumstances affecting a booking, and must manage urgent guest maintenance issues reasonably and promptly.

13. Property information, photographs and intellectual property

By providing property information, links, descriptions or photographs, the Property Partner confirms it has permission to provide and license that material to PCCO for advertising the accommodation. PCCO may copy, resize, optimise and display approved property information and photos for the purpose of promoting the property and arranging enquiries and bookings, including where the information is already publicly displayed by the Property Partner on Airbnb or Booking.com.

Photographs, property branding, logos and other content remain owned by their existing rights holders; the agreement grants PCCO a limited marketing licence, not ownership. The Property Partner is responsible for any third-party photography permissions it grants.

14. Accuracy and unauthorised representations

The Property Partner will use reasonable care to ensure that photographs, bedrooms, guest capacity, parking, pricing, amenities and other property details it supplies are accurate and current, and will promptly correct significant inaccuracies.

The Property Partner is not responsible for incorrect, unapproved representations, promises or contractual commitments made solely by PCCO without the Property Partner's authorisation. Likewise PCCO is not responsible for inaccurate property claims independently supplied by the Property Partner. Any material variation from the agreed booking must be approved by the affected party before it is promised.

15. Ending the partnership

Either party may end the PCCO Stays property partnership on 30 days' written notice without an additional termination fee. Notice may be given by email to the relevant business contact address, including partners@thepropertycareco.co.uk for PCCO.

Termination does not cancel confirmed guest bookings automatically, does not remove outstanding payment or commission obligations for commissionable bookings already confirmed, and does not authorise either party to disregard obligations to existing guests. The parties will cooperate reasonably to complete or responsibly resolve such bookings.

16. Removal of advertising after termination

PCCO will stop actively promoting and remove the Property Partner's live listings and promotional use of the relevant property photographs and branding within 14 days after termination takes effect, subject to the minimum information required for confirmed bookings, legal obligations, financial records and reasonable archival requirements. PCCO will not use the Property Partner's materials for new marketing after that date.

The Property Partner may request confirmation of removal by writing to partners@thepropertycareco.co.uk. This clause does not require deletion of legally required records or private evidence of historical transactions.

17. VAT registration, booking statements and invoices

PCCO confirms to the Property Partner that The Property Care Co. is VAT registered. PCCO will issue an appropriate VAT invoice for commission charged and a detailed booking statement or settlement document for every booking arranged through PCCO that is completed and financially settled.

The statement will identify the booking value, any separately excluded cleaning fee, PCCO commission at 7.85%, VAT on that commission, any applicable 1.35% credit-card processing charge, adjustments and refunds, guest funds actually received and the net balance due to the Property Partner. VAT will be shown in accordance with the applicable law. The Property Partner remains responsible for its own accommodation invoices and taxes where applicable.

18. Information required for listing

To begin listing The Windsor Nook, PCCO ordinarily requests only the property's trading or listing name, suitable property photographs or approved links, and the Property Partner's business contact details. PCCO may obtain publicly displayed property descriptions, features, amenities, locations and other listing information from property links provided by the Property Partner, subject to rights and approval.

Additional information reasonably necessary for an actual booking, payment, compliance, dispute or legal obligation may be requested separately. PCCO does not require unrelated confidential business records simply to advertise a property, and will publish only approved listing material and business contact information intended to be public.

19. Private documents, files and access

Private documents supplied for partner onboarding are held in PCCO's internal file storage within a password-protected ZIP archive, rather than being placed on the public property listing. PCCO limits access to personnel who need the documents to administer the relationship and will take reasonable steps to protect them from unauthorised access, loss and disclosure.

Password protection does not replace PCCO's legal duties regarding secure handling, purpose limitation, retention or breach response. Any actual change to the storage arrangements must continue to meet those duties. PCCO will not sell confidential onboarding documents to third parties.

20. Payment details and financial information

PCCO does not ask guests to provide online banking passwords, PINs, security answers or full card details directly to PCCO, and does not store guests' card numbers or payment credentials in partner files. Guest card payments, where accepted, are processed by the appointed payment provider.

PCCO may need the Property Partner's bank account details to make an agreed bank transfer and must handle any such details confidentially, only for legitimate settlement and records purposes. PCCO will never request the Property Partner's banking password, PIN or account login credentials. Booking values, invoices and settlement records are necessarily retained as business and accounting records; a promise not to retain any financial information would be inconsistent with those obligations.

21. Data handling, privacy and retention

PCCO uses the Property Partner's approved listing and business contact data to advertise accommodation, respond to enquiries, introduce guests, coordinate bookings, process agreed payments, administer the relationship and meet legal and accounting obligations. Depending on the activity, this processing is based on contractual necessity, legal obligations and, where appropriate, PCCO's legitimate business interests. Information may be shared where necessary with prospective customers, payment processors, operational service providers and professional advisers subject to appropriate safeguards; it is not sold as an unrelated contact list.

Approved listing information may already be publicly accessible via Airbnb, Booking.com or a property website. PCCO will not treat all publicly available information as free from data-protection obligations. Private onboarding documents are not disclosed to guests as public listings. Personal data is retained only as long as needed for the relevant purposes, statutory record-keeping, and legitimate dispute or claim periods, then securely deleted or anonymised as appropriate.

Individuals can contact partners@thepropertycareco.co.uk to request access, correction, erasure where applicable, restriction or other relevant UK GDPR rights, or to raise a privacy complaint. They may also contact the UK Information Commissioner's Office. If information is processed outside the UK, PCCO must ensure lawful transfer arrangements apply. This clause supplements, and does not replace, any separate privacy notice PCCO is legally required to provide.

22. Acceptance, changes and the separate website agreement

These terms are a bespoke set for Dimi Boutique Stays Ltd and The Windsor Nook, incorporating the original PCCO Property Partner Terms sent on 13 November 2026 together with the subsequently agreed clarifications. This version, 2026-11-13-dimi-v1, supersedes the former terms only for Dimi Boutique Stays Ltd when accepted; other PCCO partners' agreements are unaffected. No change to confirmed booking terms takes effect without the parties' written agreement.

Acceptance is made by the authorised Property Partner representative through a private invitation linked to its partner record, by ticking the acceptance checkbox and selecting Accept Partner Terms. PCCO records the partner, company, email, date, time and exact Terms version. The separate PCCO Host Solutions direct-booking website service, its setup fee and monthly charges are governed only by its own service agreement and are not created or amended by accepting these PCCO Stays terms.
