LANDLORD FAQ

Before we manage it.

The practical details behind the PCCO model.

Is this an AST?

PCCO’s model is designed around managed short and medium-term guest accommodation rather than a conventional long-term AST. The exact legal, planning, lease, mortgage and insurance position depends on the property and remains subject to the relevant rules and permissions.

When do I get paid?

Owner proceeds from cleared accommodation income are reconciled and paid fortnightly after deducting the agreed PCCO management fee and any agreed property operating costs.

Can I stop the service?

Yes. Our intended management model uses 30 days’ written notice. We then stop accepting new bookings beyond the termination date. Existing confirmed bookings must be honoured or otherwise agreed, and early cancellation or relocation costs may apply if the property is required back sooner.

Who pays the bills?

The landlord remains responsible for property bills such as gas, electricity, water, broadband, council tax where applicable, insurance and ownership or lease costs.

Who pays for major maintenance?

The landlord remains responsible for major electrical, plumbing, boiler/heating, structural and similar property maintenance under both packages.

What is different about the 20% package?

Both packages receive the same full management. The 20% + VAT option adds qualifying everyday property-care items such as minor furniture repairs, handles, blind/curtain repairs, light bulbs and qualifying small appliances or furnishings costing up to £150 per item, subject to fair-use terms.

Are cleaning, linen and consumables managed?

Yes. PCCO coordinates cleaning, linen and replenishment in both packages. The management fee covers the management service; underlying property operating costs are handled separately unless they are charged to the guest or otherwise agreed.

Is income guaranteed?

No. Revenue and occupancy depend on the property, location, market demand, pricing, seasonality and availability. Any revenue figures shown on this page are illustrative examples, not guaranteed returns.
