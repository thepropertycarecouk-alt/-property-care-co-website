Accommodation built around the job

Project accommodation has to work around real operational requirements. Teams may need separate beds, van or car parking, an equipped kitchen, Wi-Fi, laundry facilities, flexible arrival dates and the ability to extend when a programme moves. We source around those details rather than asking your team to fit a standard hotel booking.

For short projects and longer placements

PCCO Stays can help with a single worker, a small crew or a requirement spread across several nearby properties. We work with furnished houses, apartments and other suitable accommodation for short, medium and extended stays. Availability, price, cleaning and booking terms are confirmed for each requirement before anything is agreed.

What to send us

The fastest brief is simple: worksite postcode or area, check-in date, expected length of stay, number of guests, bedroom or bed requirements and how many vehicles need parking. If dates are still moving, send the expected window and we can work from that.

Why contractors use furnished accommodation

For teams working away, a furnished property can provide more useful living space than separate hotel rooms. A kitchen, washing facilities and shared living areas can make longer assignments more practical, while company buyers can keep one accommodation contact instead of managing multiple individual bookings.

Parking, beds and commute matter

For contractor stays, the cheapest property is not always the most useful one. A property closer to site, with the right bed configuration and suitable vehicle parking, can reduce wasted travel time and make the stay easier for the team. Tell us what is essential and what is flexible so we can compare the right options.
