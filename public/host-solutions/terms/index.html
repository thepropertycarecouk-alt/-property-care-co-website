Website deposit & cancellation terms

Transparent, all-in pricing. A £99 one-time deposit. No automatic subscription.

Our business

The Property Care Co., 14 Vicarage Road, Stourbridge, DY8 4JE, United Kingdom. Contact partners@thepropertycareco.co.uk or +44 7411 251361.

Deposit and website prices

Your £99 deposit is a part-payment towards your chosen website build, not an additional fee. Opening build prices apply to qualifying new orders placed by 31 December 2026 inclusive: Starter £299.25 (balance £200.25), Direct Booking £487.50 (balance £388.50), Portfolio Pro £746.25 (balance £647.25). The standard build prices are £399, £650 and £995 respectively. The listed website package prices are final; separately requested extras are quoted and agreed in advance.

Refunds and cancellation

Full refund within 14 days: Request it by emailing partners@thepropertycareco.co.uk within 14 calendar days of your deposit payment. We will refund the complete £99 to your original payment method. Your card provider may take additional time to display the credit. If we cannot agree or deliver the service, we will refund your deposit. After this period, any further cancellation conditions are detailed in the separately agreed project contract; statutory rights remain unaffected.

Scope and remaining balance

After payment, our team will discuss your goals and prepare an agreed project brief. Remaining build balance and payment milestones are agreed in writing before chargeable development starts; no additional payment is automatically collected. A deposit alone does not authorise website work or guarantee a delivery date. Any third-party software, premium themes or licences are quoted separately before you commit.

Six months of maintenance savings

Monthly maintenance is optional under a separate agreement: Starter £14/month for the first six paid months from activation, then £19/month; Direct Booking £34/month for six paid months, then £39/month; Portfolio Pro £50/month for six paid months, then £55/month. The deposit does not create any subscription. Maintenance, support scope and cancellation terms are confirmed separately before billing begins.

AI allowance and separate services

Standalone AI costs £150 setup and £20/month for up to 1,000 assistant text replies/month. Optional Direct Booking AI costs £75 setup and £10/month for up to 500 replies/month. Portfolio Pro includes a standard AI assistant with up to 500 replies/month. The initial 14-day trial covers 50 replies, without automatic paid continuation. Extra usage pauses until you agree any upgrade; no surprise overages. Voice AI, WhatsApp, bespoke PMS integration and related third-party fees require separate scope and pricing. AI must be activated separately; the demo on this site is illustrative.

Standalone AI setup and monthly service

The standalone text-based AI assistant has a separate one-time £150 setup charge. That payment does not subscribe you to the £20/month service. We confirm your requirements, approved information, implementation scope and expected launch before starting work. You must separately authorise the monthly subscription before recurring billing begins. Extra usage and integrations are not charged automatically. For cancellation or refund requests regarding setup, email partners@thepropertycareco.co.uk; your statutory rights are unaffected.

Manage subscriptions

When you authorise an AI or website maintenance subscription using its separate Stripe checkout, charges recur monthly until cancellation. You can review invoices, update your card and cancel future renewals at the secure Stripe billing portal. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid billing period. Maintenance customers eligible for the opening offer receive £5 off for the first six monthly billing periods with the opening discount applied at activation, then standard pricing applies. Please ask our team to verify the discount on the Stripe checkout before subscribing.

Consultation form and communication

When available, the consultation form sends the information you explicitly submit securely to our business enquiry address using our contracted email delivery provider. We use your details only to respond to the request, not for unsolicited marketing. If direct delivery cannot be confirmed, the website presents a prepared email for you to send yourself; it does not claim that an unsuccessful submission was received. We do not automatically send marketing or sales follow-up emails.

Stripe payment and your data

Stripe securely processes card payments and collects your contact and business details. Stripe confirms payment on its own hosted page. Email partners@thepropertycareco.co.uk to arrange a consultation or request a refund. We use payment and enquiry data to provide the requested service and process refunds; we do not treat a payment redirect as evidence of a successful charge.
