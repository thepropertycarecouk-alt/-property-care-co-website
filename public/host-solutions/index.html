QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Before you get started.

Is the initial mockup really free?

Yes. We offer an initial homepage design direction and planning brief without a design fee. A full website build is a separate paid service.

Do I have to buy a website afterwards?

No. You can review the initial recommendation and decide whether to proceed. There is no obligation to purchase.

Can I keep my existing booking engine?

Potentially. We will review which booking engine or channel manager you already use and what integration options it supports. Third-party subscription fees remain your responsibility.

Can I request the AI assistant without a website?

Yes. The AI assistant can be offered as a separate service for existing websites, subject to the site's technical capabilities and data integration options.
