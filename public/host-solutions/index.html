Good questions. Clear answers.

Before we get started.

Who owns the website?

Ownership, access and handover are recorded in your project agreement before payment. We will distinguish your content and domain from third-party tools and any licensed components. Final transfer and payment conditions must be agreed in writing.

What does maintenance include?

Secure hosting, SSL and routine technical upkeep within the agreed service scope. Content-change allowances, support response targets and exclusions are confirmed in your agreement; Portfolio Pro includes agreed content maintenance allowances. Major redesigns and bespoke development are separate.

Can I keep my booking engine or channel manager?

We first verify the integration supported by your platform. Standard setup may be an embed, link or existing connector. Third-party subscriptions are not included. Bespoke development is separately scoped. We never promise live calendar availability without a verified connection.

What happens after the 14-day AI trial?

Starter and Direct Booking trials expire without automatic charges. You must explicitly agree to paid continuation. Direct Booking has an optional £75 setup + £10/month AI add-on; Starter can discuss the standalone £150 setup + £20/month service. Portfolio Pro includes standard AI subject to an agreed fair-use allowance.

How do the opening prices work?

Orders placed by 31 December 2026 qualify for 25% off the one-off website build. The £5/month maintenance saving lasts for the first six paid months after separately agreed activation, then the standard monthly rate applies. Paying the deposit does not start any subscription. Standalone AI and co-hosting are not discounted.

How does the £99 deposit work?

£99 is part of your website build total, not an extra fee. Remaining offer build balances: £200.25 for Starter, £388.50 for Direct Booking, £647.25 for Portfolio Pro. A full refund is available if you request it within 14 days of paying. We agree scope and remaining payment milestones separately before any chargeable build work. No VAT charged.

Will VAT be added to these prices?

No. PCCO is not UK VAT-registered. All displayed prices are final amounts and no VAT is charged or added. We do not issue VAT invoices. If our registration status changes, we will update the terms for future orders.

Can I cancel, and is the deposit refundable?

Yes. Email partners@thepropertycareco.co.uk within 14 calendar days after your deposit payment for a full £99 refund. If we cannot agree or deliver the service, we also refund your deposit. Further cancellation arrangements for a commissioned build are set out in writing before work starts. Your statutory rights are unaffected.

How many design revisions are included?

The free offer includes one initial homepage mockup and two proposed design directions, not unlimited design work. Paid revision rounds and acceptance milestones are agreed in the project brief before the build begins.

What if I have more properties?

Starter and Direct Booking cover up to five; Portfolio Pro covers up to ten. We can scope a larger portfolio during consultation. Extra listings, locations and custom features are quoted before work begins.

What are the AI and support limits?

Standalone AI includes 1,000 text replies/month; the Direct Booking add-on and Portfolio Pro inclusion each allow 500 replies/month. The 14-day trial offers 50 replies. No automatic overages: use beyond the allowance pauses until separately agreed. The assistant shown here is a scripted demonstration, not an activated service.

How do I send a free consultation request?

Complete the form, then open the prepared enquiry to partners@thepropertycareco.co.uk and press Send in your email app. The website does not transmit the message by itself. You can also copy your brief into a new email.
