Corporate accommodation
that fits the assignment.

Furnished accommodation for employees, consultants and business travellers on short or extended assignments across the UK. Share the location, dates, guest numbers and practical requirements and PCCO Stays will coordinate suitable options.

More practical than a long hotel stay

Corporate stays often need a proper kitchen, laundry, Wi-Fi and enough space to live as well as work. Furnished apartments and houses can be especially useful for multi-week and multi-month assignments where a standard hotel room becomes restrictive.

One accommodation contact

Give us the brief once and we can work across our network. This is useful when a company has several travellers, changing dates or multiple locations and wants one team coordinating the accommodation requirement.

Flexible requirements

Tell us what matters: commute, parking, bedrooms, workspace, cleaning frequency, billing requirements and whether the stay may need to extend. We use those details to shortlist suitable accommodation rather than simply returning the closest available property.
