RELOCATION ACCOMMODATION · UK-WIDE

Relocation accommodation
while the next move takes shape.

Furnished temporary accommodation for employees, families and individuals relocating within the UK. We can work from the destination, expected dates, household size and practical requirements while a permanent home or longer-term arrangement is being secured.

A furnished base from day one

Relocation accommodation should make the transition easier. A functioning kitchen, Wi-Fi, laundry facilities and enough space for the household can make temporary stays more comfortable while permanent housing, schools, work locations and other arrangements are finalised.

Short, medium and extended stays

Some relocations need only a few weeks; others take longer. We can work with indicative dates and discuss extension options with accommodation partners where required. Availability and final terms are always confirmed for the individual stay.

Requirements beyond the postcode

Location matters, but so do bedrooms, parking, pets, accessibility, transport links and the needs of the household. Share the details that are important and we can search around them.

Tell us where the move is taking you

Send the destination, dates, household size and any must-have requirements. We can source from the displayed portfolio and wider UK network.
